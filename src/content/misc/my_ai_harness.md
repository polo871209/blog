## Extensible

If I had to rank the best coding agents out there, no doubt, it'd be OpenCode. Its TUI feels like a GUI, responsive and never gives me any trouble. Sensible defaults, and the experience is incomparable to most agents. I understand people compare different minimal or fast agents, and usually OpenCode isn't the fastest (well, still faster than Claude Code), but the features it provides are outstanding.

However, when it come to extensibility there is where OpenCode have something to catch up.
